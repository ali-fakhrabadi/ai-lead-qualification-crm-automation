## Platform fee

Upwork's Freelancer Service Fee is 0% to 15% per contract, fixed when the contract starts
(Upwork Help, "Learn about the Freelancer Service Fee", read 2026-11-19). Prices below are set so
that the net stays at or above the $30/hour baseline even at 15%: a gross of about $35/hour.

## What this means for the offer

- The starter market for "an n8n workflow that qualifies leads" is crowded and cheap. Competing
  on price would mean under-pricing tested, documented work.
- What most listings do not show, and this project can prove: idempotent intake and replay
  safety, deterministic rules around the model, a self-hosted model option, human approval
  before anything is sent, retry/error workflows tested in a harness, and a measured benchmark.
- So the packages are priced from estimated hours at the baseline, with scope written
  narrowly (one source, one CRM, defined limits) and exclusions stated.
